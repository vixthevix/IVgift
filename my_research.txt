0100 0000 0100 0000

0100 - byte 0-1, gift type.
We only care about: Pokemon, Pokemon Egg, Item (0100, 0200, 0300)

0100 - byte 4-5, gift type specific data
Pokemon - is the receiver the OT of the gift pokemon? (0000 for yes, 0100 for no)
Egg - same as Pokemon.
Item - item id (from 0 to FFFF).
